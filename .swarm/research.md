# Research

Objective: make an android app of swarm ai

## Findings
- **competitors** — Gemini mobile app functions as an overlay assistant on Android devices. [1]
- **competitors** — Kimi app is available through its website and a dedicated mobile app. [2]
- **features** — Gemini architecture can process and generate text, computer code, images, audio, and video simultaneously. [1]
- **features** — Gemini offers efficient on‑device Nano and high‑throughput Flash variants. [1]
- **features** — Gemini’s extended context windows enable analysis of large datasets such as entire codebases or long‑form videos in a single prompt. [1]
- **features** — Kimi K3 is a 2.8 trillion‑parameter open‑weights model that supports multimodal image and video input. [2]
- **features** — Kimi’s Swarm feature utilizes hundreds of sub‑agents for deep research tasks. [2]
- **features** — Kimi‑Dev is a 72B‑parameter coding‑focused model released in June 2025. [2]
- **features** — Kimi‑Researcher is an autonomous AI research agent available through Kimi’s website and mobile app. [2]
- **pricing** — Kimi K3’s custom license requires revenue sharing of up to 30% for inference providers generating over US$20 million annually. [2]
- **audience** — Gemini is integrated into the Google ecosystem, targeting Android users as an overlay assistant. [1]
- **audience** — Kimi targets the Chinese market and is used by developers and researchers for large‑scale AI tasks. [2]
- **trends** — Open‑weights models like Kimi K3 are gaining prominence in China’s AI industry. [2]
- **trends** — Gemini’s efficient on‑device Nano variant caters to low‑resource devices. [1]
- **trends** — Gemini’s high‑throughput Flash variant supports high‑volume use cases. [1]
